Get cookies from your browser
How to copy cookies from your browser
  1. Log in to the site in Chrome or Firefox.
  2. Press F12 → Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox).
  3. Click Cookies → select the site domain.
  4. Copy each cookie's Name and Value.
  5. Paste here as name=value; name2=value2.

Only use cookies from sites you own or have permission to access.
